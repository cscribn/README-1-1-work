# README-1-1-work

## Notes

### Initial Frequency

* For embedded team reports: 30 minutes every 2 weeks
* For off-team reports: 30 minutes every 1 week

## Agendas

### Manager Themes

* Check in/catch up
* Review incomplete TODOs
* Your topics/what can I help with?
* Talent: who is critical, needs more work, has high bus factor? Who doesn't fit, resists change, underperforms?
* Team health: sprint goals, quarterly initiatives, code/PR/ticket metrics
* Career development: completed accomplishments, goal progress

### Lead/Principal Themes

* Check in/catch up
* Review incomplete TODOs
* Your topics/what can I help with?
* Team health: sprint goals, quarterly initiatives, code/PR/ticket metrics
* Career development: completed accomplishments, goal progress

### Skip-Level Themes

* Check in/catch up
* Your topics/what can I help with?
* Career development: what are you working on, completed accomplishments, goal progress
* Team health/process optimization: AI, collaboration, friction, workload

### Individual Contributor Themes

* Check in/catch up
* Review incomplete TODOs
* Your topics/what can I help with?
* Career development: completed accomplishments, goal progress
* Developer health: code/PR/ticket metrics

### Off-Team Peer Agenda

* Introductions (5 min): backgrounds, current role, & focus areas.
* High level product & project overviews (15 min)
  * What's working well for your team?
  * Where are you seeing friction?
* Explore intersections (5 min): overlaps or opportunities to accelerate each other's work.
* Capture actions.

### On-Team Peer Themes

* Check in/catch up
* Team progress: sprint goals, quarterly initiatives
* Career development: recent learning, goal progress
